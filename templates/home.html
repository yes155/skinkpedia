Editorial standards

Evidence should match the risk of the claim

Skinkpedia prioritizes peer-reviewed research, veterinary sources, universities, zoological institutions, government wildlife agencies and recognized welfare organizations. Commercial care sheets are used cautiously and should not serve as the sole authority for medical treatment or high-risk welfare claims.

Researchers whose published work is cited are not presented as Skinkpedia staff, advisors or endorsers unless a real relationship is separately verified.
