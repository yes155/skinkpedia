# Affiliate Disclosure

**Skinkpedia may earn a commission when readers purchase products through qualifying affiliate links.**

If a link is an affiliate link, the purchase price should not increase because of the commission.

## Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not change the factual standard used in Skinkpedia's care content.

A product category should appear because it supports an established husbandry requirement—not because a commission is available.

For example, if species-specific evidence establishes the need to measure a basking surface, a suitable infrared thermometer may be discussed. The product does not create the biological requirement.

## What affiliate recommendations may cover

Commercial links may be used for products such as:

- enclosures;
- thermostats;
- thermometers;
- hygrometers;
- UVB equipment;
- substrate;
- hides;
- feeding equipment;
- other husbandry supplies.

Products should be matched to the animal and setup described on the page.

## Prices and availability

Prices, stock and product specifications can change.

Any price shown on Skinkpedia should be treated as a dated example unless the site retrieves live pricing directly.
