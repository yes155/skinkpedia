# Moniqua Nelson-Tunley

**Moniqua Nelson-Tunley provides editorial review for Skinkpedia's skink content.**

Her review role helps keep Skinkpedia's articles biologically careful, evidence-led and clear about the difference between general skink information, species-specific care, wildlife identification and conservation context.

**Role:** Editorial Reviewer

**Focus:** Skink biology, conservation context, source accuracy, taxonomy-sensitive wording and evidence boundaries.

## Public research background

Moniqua Nelson-Tunley is listed in public academic records as the author of a Massey University MSc thesis on population fragmentation in the small-scaled skink (*Oligosoma microlepis*), including the consequences of landscape transformation for distribution, morphology and genetics.

She is also listed as a co-author of research on genetic diversity and gene flow in the small-scaled skink in fragmented volcanic landscapes.

## Review scope on Skinkpedia

Editorial review may include checking whether an article:

- identifies the correct skink species, genus or family;
- avoids treating one species' care values as universal;
- separates confirmed facts from hypotheses or uncertain records;
- uses the right evidence strength for health, conservation and taxonomy claims;
- avoids overstating commercial care-sheet claims;
- explains scientific details in language useful to keepers and general readers.

## Boundaries

Skinkpedia does not present editorial review as a substitute for veterinary diagnosis, professional wildlife-permit advice or direct field identification by a local authority.

Health pages remain educational. Urgent illness, injury or welfare concerns should be handled by a qualified reptile or exotic-animal veterinarian.
