# About Skinkpedia

**Skinkpedia publishes practical skink care guides, checklists and reference pages.** The site is designed to answer the questions keepers actually ask: how to set up a safe enclosure, what to feed, how to spot common warning signs, which species they may have seen, and when to seek help from an exotic-reptile veterinarian or wildlife professional.

Skinkpedia covers both captive-care and natural-history topics, but it keeps those jobs separate. A pet-care page should help a keeper make safer husbandry decisions. A wild-skink page should help with identification, habitat, range and coexistence. A biology page should explain anatomy, evolution, taxonomy or behavior without turning every fact into a pet-care rule.

## What Skinkpedia covers

Skinkpedia is organized around several connected subject areas:

- skink taxonomy, anatomy and natural history;
- wild-skink identification and regional distribution;
- pet-skink species selection;
- enclosure, diet, lighting, heating and humidity;
- handling, enrichment and general health monitoring;
- backyard coexistence and humane exclusion;
- species-specific guides for blue-tongue, crocodile and other skinks.

Exact care requirements vary by species and locality. Skinkpedia therefore separates broad family-level guidance from species-specific husbandry because temperature, humidity, enclosure size, diet and lighting values should not be transferred from one species to another without evidence.

## How our articles are developed

Articles are built from a structured topical map so that each URL has a defined subject and does not unnecessarily duplicate nearby pages.

During research and editing, Skinkpedia prioritizes:

1. peer-reviewed research;
2. veterinary guidance;
3. universities and academic institutions;
4. zoos and government wildlife agencies;
5. recognized conservation organizations;
6. specialist husbandry references;
7. commercial product specifications only when the product itself is being discussed.

Exact numerical care recommendations are kept at the species level wherever possible.

See the [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/) and [Sources & Research Methodology](/sources-research-methodology/) to understand how the site is maintained.

## What Skinkpedia is not

Skinkpedia is not a veterinary clinic and does not diagnose or treat animals.

Health articles are designed to help readers:

- recognize husbandry-related risk factors;
- notice warning signs;
- understand when home observation is reasonable;
- know when a reptile veterinarian or wildlife professional should be contacted.
