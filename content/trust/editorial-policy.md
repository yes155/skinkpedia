# Editorial Policy

**Skinkpedia publishes evidence-led educational content about skinks and aims to keep factual precision, species identity and reader safety ahead of search traffic or commercial value.**

Research and writing are handled by [Farrukh Abdullah](/authors/farrukh-abdullah/). Editorial review is handled by [Moniqua Nelson-Tunley](/editors/moniqua-nelson-tunley/).

## Editorial roles

**Researcher & Writer:** Farrukh Abdullah researches, writes and maintains Skinkpedia articles, topical structure, internal links and publication updates.

**Editorial Reviewer:** Moniqua Nelson-Tunley reviews Skinkpedia's skink content for biological care, source accuracy, taxonomy-sensitive wording and appropriate evidence boundaries.

Skinkpedia does not present either role as a substitute for veterinary diagnosis, wildlife-permit advice or emergency animal care.

## How pages show responsibility

Every main article displays a compact editorial line near the title with:

- the named writer;
- the named reviewer;
- linked contributor profile pages;
- a visible publication date;
- a visible update date.

Author and reviewer profile pages include a role summary, scope of review, public profile links where available and structured data that identifies the person behind the contribution.

Trust pages such as About, Sources, Corrections, Privacy, Affiliate Disclosure and Contact explain who is responsible for the site, how content is made, how corrections are handled and how readers can reach the publisher.

## Page ownership and topic boundaries

Each article has a defined subject.

A general skink-care article may explain that temperature, UVB, humidity and diet matter, but it should not present one species' exact values as universal for Scincidae.

Species guides own species-specific details such as:

- enclosure dimensions;
- thermal gradients;
- humidity;
- UVB exposure;
- diet proportions;
- lifespan;
- locality differences.

This reduces contradiction and prevents care values from being copied into the wrong biological context.

## Evidence hierarchy

When sources disagree, Skinkpedia generally gives greater weight to:

1. peer-reviewed research;
2. reptile-veterinary guidance;
3. universities and academic institutions;
4. zoos and government wildlife agencies;
5. recognized conservation organizations;
6. specialist husbandry sources;
7. commercial sources for product specifications or clearly identified husbandry context;
8. community anecdotes only when explicitly labeled as anecdotal.

A lower-ranked source can still be useful when it provides direct, species-specific husbandry information that stronger sources do not cover. In that case, the claim should be presented at the strength the evidence supports.

## Exact numbers

Exact values require extra care.

Before publishing a specific number, editors should confirm:

- the correct species;
- life stage;
- sex or locality where relevant;
- measurement type, such as total length vs snout-to-vent length;
- whether a temperature is surface temperature or air temperature;
- whether a UV value is measured UVI rather than bulb percentage;
- whether the source presents the value as a requirement, recommendation or observed range.

If those conditions cannot be established, the wording should be made less precise rather than supplying an invented number.

## Health and safety content

Skinkpedia does not diagnose disease.

Health content may explain:

- observable signs;
- husbandry risk factors;
- routine prevention;
- safe first-response steps;
- when veterinary attention is appropriate.

It should not invent diagnoses, drug dosages, prescription treatments or unsafe procedures.

## Conservation and legal content

Conservation status and wildlife law are time-sensitive.

Where a page discusses a protected species, current government or recognized conservation sources should be checked before publication and during later updates.

Readers should verify local rules before collecting, transporting or keeping wild reptiles.

## Commercial content

Affiliate potential does not determine factual conclusions.

Product recommendations should be tied to a previously established biological requirement, for example:

> the animal needs a measured basking gradient → recommend a suitable thermostat or thermometer category.

The reverse approach is not acceptable:

> a product is profitable → create a care requirement to justify it.

## AI-assisted workflow

Skinkpedia may use AI tools during research organization, drafting, editing, quality assurance or technical production.

AI-generated output is not treated as a source. Factual claims must be supported by the underlying evidence, and publication decisions remain an editorial responsibility.

## Updating articles

An article may be updated when:

- taxonomy changes;
- a legal or conservation status changes;
- stronger husbandry guidance becomes available;
- a product or price becomes outdated;
- a factual error is identified;
- the page can answer its existing user task more clearly.

Major corrections are handled under the [Corrections Policy](/corrections-policy/).
