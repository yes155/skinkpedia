# Contact Skinkpedia

Use this page to contact Skinkpedia about:

- factual corrections;
- source suggestions;
- broken links;
- accessibility problems;
- image or attribution concerns;
- editorial questions;
- commercial or affiliate inquiries.

## Reporting a factual error

Please include:

1. the article URL;
2. the statement you are questioning;
3. why you believe it is inaccurate;
4. a supporting source, if available.

See the [Corrections Policy](/corrections-policy/) for how correction requests are handled.

## Veterinary questions

Skinkpedia cannot diagnose an individual animal or replace a reptile veterinarian.

If a skink has severe breathing difficulty, uncontrolled bleeding, serious trauma, marked weakness, seizures, major swelling or another urgent clinical problem, contact a qualified reptile/exotics veterinarian.

## Wildlife emergencies

For injured or protected wild reptiles, contact an appropriate local wildlife authority or licensed wildlife rehabilitator rather than attempting long-term treatment yourself.
