## Reporting a factual error

Please include:

1. the article URL;
2. the statement you are questioning;
3. why you believe it is inaccurate;
4. a supporting source, if available.

See the [Corrections Policy](/corrections-policy/) for how correction requests are handled.

## Source suggestions

Skinkpedia welcomes stronger sources, especially peer-reviewed research, veterinary guidance, government wildlife pages, zoo husbandry material, conservation documents and species-specific care information.

Please include the source title, author or organization, publication date where available, and the Skinkpedia page where you think it should be used.

## Veterinary questions

Skinkpedia cannot diagnose an individual animal or replace a reptile veterinarian.

If a skink has severe breathing difficulty, uncontrolled bleeding, serious trauma, marked weakness, seizures, major swelling or another urgent clinical problem, contact a qualified reptile or exotics veterinarian.

## Wildlife emergencies

For injured or protected wild reptiles, contact an appropriate local wildlife authority or licensed wildlife rehabilitator rather than attempting long-term treatment yourself.
