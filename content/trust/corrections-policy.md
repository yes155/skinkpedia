# Corrections Policy

**Skinkpedia corrects factual errors when they are identified and distinguishes between routine updates and substantive corrections.**

## Routine updates

Routine updates can include:

- clearer wording;
- formatting improvements;
- refreshed links;
- updated product availability;
- current prices;
- newly accepted scientific names;
- added references that do not change the article's conclusion.

These changes may be reflected in the article's updated date without a separate correction note.

## Substantive corrections

A visible correction note may be appropriate when an earlier version contained a material error involving:

- species identity;
- husbandry values;
- veterinary or safety advice;
- legal status;
- conservation status;
- taxonomy that changes the meaning of the article;
- a commercial claim that could materially affect a buying decision.

Where practical, the note should state what was changed and the date of correction.

## How to report an error

Readers can report a possible factual problem through the [Contact page](/contact/).

A useful correction report includes:

- the article URL;
- the sentence or claim in question;
- the reason it may be wrong;
- a source or citation, if available.

## Review process

A correction request is evaluated against the strongest relevant evidence available.

Skinkpedia does not change an article merely because one source disagrees. The source's authority, species match, date, methodology and context are considered before a revision is made.
