# Privacy Policy

**Skinkpedia is an educational website about skink care, identification, biology and natural history.** This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when someone uses the site or contacts Skinkpedia.

## Information you provide

Skinkpedia may receive personal information if you choose to send it, for example by emailing us with a correction, source suggestion, image concern, accessibility issue or business inquiry.

That information may include your name, email address, message content and any details you choose to include.

## Website logs and hosting data

Like most websites, Skinkpedia's hosting and security providers may process technical data needed to serve the site and protect it from abuse. This can include IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and basic diagnostic logs.

Skinkpedia uses Cloudflare Workers for deployment and hosting. Cloudflare may process traffic and security data according to its own policies and service configuration.

## Analytics and cookies

Skinkpedia may use basic analytics or platform diagnostics to understand page performance, broken pages and broad usage patterns. The site should not require readers to create an account to access educational content.

If analytics, advertising or affiliate systems are added, they may use cookies or similar technologies. Readers can control cookies through their browser settings.

## Affiliate links

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If a reader clicks an affiliate link, the destination site or affiliate network may receive information needed to track the referral. Skinkpedia's [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) explains how commercial relationships are handled editorially.

## Third-party websites

Skinkpedia may link to external sources, organizations, veterinary resources, government pages, product pages or social profiles. External websites have their own privacy practices. Skinkpedia does not control those third-party policies.

## Children

Skinkpedia is not designed to collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information, they can contact us and ask for it to be deleted.

## Data retention

Messages sent to Skinkpedia may be kept as long as needed to respond, document corrections, handle disputes, improve editorial quality or meet legal and operational needs.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's features, analytics, hosting setup or legal requirements change.
