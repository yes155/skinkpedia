# Emerald Tree Skink Guide: Care, Enclosure, Diet, Size & Lifespan

**The Emerald Tree Skink (*Lamprolepis smaragdina*) is an arboreal, diurnal Southeast Asian skink that grows to roughly 8.5–10 inches (22–25 cm).** For a broader comparison of pet-skink size, temperament and care difficulty, see the [Pet Skinks guide](/pet-skinks/). Its enclosure should emphasize usable height, branches, foliage, bright light, UVB, warm upper basking areas, high humidity with ventilation, and a varied insect-based diet.

## Size, lifespan and natural lifestyle

Current species-specific guidance gives:

| Attribute | Practical value |
| --- | --- |
| **Adult total length** | About **8.5–10 in (22–25 cm)** |
| **Captive lifespan** | **At least about 7 years** with good care |
| **Lifestyle** | Arboreal and diurnal |
| **Diet** | Primarily insectivorous |

The species occurs widely in parts of Southeast Asia and the Pacific and is strongly associated with tree trunks and elevated forest structure.

## Vertical enclosure design

**A single adult should have at least about 24 × 18 × 36 inches (60 × 45 × 90 cm), with larger and especially taller enclosures preferred.**

A tall enclosure is useful only if the animal can actually use the volume. Include:

- sturdy vertical and diagonal branches;
- multiple routes between levels;
- dense visual cover;
- elevated resting places;
- safe access to heat and UVB;
- shaded retreats;
- accessible water;
- escape-proof doors or lid.

Good ventilation is essential even in a humid enclosure.

## Temperature gradient

Current ReptiFiles guidance recommends:

- **upper basking area:** about **95°F (35°C)**;
- **cooler lower zone:** about **75–85°F (24–29°C)**;
- **night:** about **68–77°F (20–25°C)**.

Because warm air rises, measure temperatures at the actual branches the skink uses rather than relying on room temperature.

## Humidity

**A daytime average around 70% with substantially higher nighttime humidity is a useful species-specific target.** ReptiFiles notes nighttime humidity can rise toward 100%.

High humidity should not mean stagnant air. The enclosure should dry somewhat during the day and maintain cross-ventilation.

## UVB and visible light

ReptiFiles recommends a measured **UVI around 2.0–3.0 at the basking branch**, with lower exposure and deep shade elsewhere.

Because this is a diurnal tree skink, bright visible illumination also matters. Use UVB and broad-spectrum lighting over part of the canopy while preserving shaded choices.

Seasonal or local day length can be simulated; current care guidance uses roughly 11–13 hours of light depending on season.

## Substrate and furnishing

Although Emerald Tree Skinks spend much of their time above ground, substrate helps with humidity, plant growth, and cushioning.

A moisture-retentive tropical substrate can be used with:

- branches and cork;
- live or artificial foliage;
- elevated hides;
- broad resting surfaces;
- safe live plants where desired.

## Diet and feeding frequency

**Emerald Tree Skinks are primarily insectivorous, with occasional fruit or flowers reported.**

Useful feeder categories include:

- crickets;
- roaches;
- locusts/grasshoppers where available;
- other appropriately sized captive invertebrates.

Current ReptiFiles guidance feeds **juveniles daily** and **adults every other day**, adjusting quantity to body condition and prey size.

Gut-load feeders and use supplements according to product directions and the skink’s UVB setup.

## Water

Keep clean water available. Misting also supplies droplets on foliage and supports the humidity cycle.

## Cohabitation

Emerald Tree Skinks can sometimes be maintained in compatible groups, but group housing is optional, not a requirement.

If more than one animal is housed together:

- increase enclosure volume;
- duplicate basking and feeding resources;
- avoid keeping males together where aggression is a concern;
- avoid mixed-sex groups unless breeding is intended;
- monitor body condition and access to resources;
- keep separation housing available.

## Frequently asked questions

### How big do Emerald Tree Skinks get?

Roughly 8.5–10 inches (22–25 cm) total length.

### How long do Emerald Tree Skinks live?

Current ReptiFiles guidance gives a captive lifespan of at least about seven years with good care.

### What size enclosure does an Emerald Tree Skink need?

At least about 24 × 18 × 36 inches (60 × 45 × 90 cm) for one adult, with larger and taller preferred.

### What temperature do Emerald Tree Skinks need?

About 95°F (35°C) at the upper basking area, with a cooler zone around 75–85°F (24–29°C).

### What humidity do they need?

Around 70% by day, with a strong nighttime rise, while maintaining good ventilation.

### What UVI should an Emerald Tree Skink have?

Current ReptiFiles guidance targets about UVI 2.0–3.0 at the basking branch, with shade available.

### What do Emerald Tree Skinks eat?

Primarily varied, appropriately sized feeder invertebrates.
